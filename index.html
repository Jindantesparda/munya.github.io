ZimVista (3D Digital Twin Platform)

ZimVista is a 3D Digital Twin platform that allows users to virtually explore real-world spaces before physically visiting them. Using 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS), a simple smartphone video can be transformed into a photorealistic, navigable environment that feels closer to a video game than a traditional virtual tour. The platform is designed for universities, hotels, Airbnbs, rentals, event venues, shopping centres, and tourism destinations, helping users understand exactly what they are paying for before arrival.

  • Converts ordinary smartphone videos into immersive 3D Gaussian Splatting environments.
  • Allows prospective tenants, Airbnb guests, and tourists to inspect locations before arrival.
  • Reduces spatial anxiety by enabling realistic virtual walkthroughs.
  • Provides accessibility assessment for wheelchair users and individuals with mobility challenges.
  • Creates digital twins for universities, businesses, hotels, and tourism destinations.
2026

HomeConnect (Diaspora Child Welfare Platform)

HomeConnect is a social impact platform designed to support Zimbabwean families separated by migration. The platform helps parents living abroad monitor the wellbeing of children left in the care of relatives while ensuring financial support reaches its intended purpose. Through collaboration with social workers, guardians, and parents, HomeConnect improves transparency, accountability, and communication, ensuring children receive the support they need despite geographical separation.

  • Supports children whose parents are working outside Zimbabwe.
  • Tracks welfare reports and living conditions through social worker assessments.
  • Provides accountability and transparency for remitted funds.
  • Enables communication between parents, guardians, and support personnel.
  • Strengthens family connections despite geographical separation.
